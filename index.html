Mod Math

-7 divided by 3 leaves -1 or 2, depending on whether the quotient rounds toward zero or down.

Boundary: arithmetic only Mod Math does whole-number remainder arithmetic on the numbers you type. It does not know how any real language, database or chip rounds a division, and it gives no verdict on which kind of remainder your code should use. Every figure is your own entry.

The sign truth

-7 divided by 3: truncated division (quotient rounds toward zero) gives quotient -2 and remainder -1, floored division (quotient rounds down) gives -3 and 2, and Euclidean division (remainder never negative) gives -3 and 2. The remainders differ, so the choice of division matters for these numbers.

The ring truth

On a ring of 12 slots (0 to 11), starting at 9 and moving 7 steps lands on slot 4, after wrapping past the end 1 time.

The match truth

17 and 5 mod 6: both leave 5 (never negative), so they land on the same slot. Their difference 12 is 2 x 6.
